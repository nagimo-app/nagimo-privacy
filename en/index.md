# Privacy Policy (nagimo)

Last updated: 2026-11-12

[日本語](../)

nagimo ("the app") respects your privacy.
This policy explains the information the app handles.

## 1. Our approach: your location and settings never leave your device
The app **does not send your home location, current location, lock settings, chosen apps, or usage time off your device**.
All of this is handled only on your device.
Separately, the app collects usage statistics to improve itself (see Section 4).

- The app has no servers of its own. The only information sent off your device is what is used for the statistics in Section 4.
- It contains no advertising, tracking, or crash reporting.
- There are no accounts or sign-ins.

## 2. Information handled on your device
- **Home location and area**: the location (latitude and longitude) and radius of the home you register.
- **Lock settings**: the apps, categories, and websites to lock at each place, and their available times and days.
- **Current state**: whether you are at home, and when a pause ends.
- **Usage milestones**: whether you have used your locked apps past milestones such as 30 minutes or 1 hour today, so the lock screen can let you know (cleared when the day changes).

This information is shared only between the app and its extensions (lock screen, schedule monitoring, and usage display), and never leaves your device.

## 3. Device permissions
- **Location (Always)**: used to detect when you enter or leave the home area you registered, and switch locks automatically. "Always" is requested so this works even when the app is closed. Your location history is not stored and is never sent off your device.
- **Screen Time**: used to lock the apps and websites you choose, and to show the usage of your locked apps. iOS only provides information about your chosen apps in anonymized form. Usage is displayed using a mechanism provided by iOS; the app does not store or send it (except for the usage milestones above).

You can revoke these permissions at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

## 4. Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)
To understand how features are used and improve them, the app uses Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics), provided by Google LLC. The following information is sent to Google's servers:

- **Actions**: that you started a pause, and the length you chose (15 minutes, 1 hour, or until tomorrow). If you end a pause before it expires, that you did so, the length you had chosen, and how many minutes it actually lasted.
- **App usage**: information Firebase Analytics records automatically, such as opening the app for the first time or starting to use it.
- **Device information**: such as device model, OS version, and language.
- **App identifier**: an identifier Firebase Analytics assigns to each installation of the app. It is not tied to your name or any account.
- **Approximate region**: the country, region, city, or similar that Google estimates from your IP address.

The following information is not sent:

- Your home location, current location, or anything else obtained through iPhone Location Services
- The apps, categories, or websites you lock
- Your Screen Time usage
- The advertising identifier (IDFA)

These statistics are not used to personalize ads, and the setting that shares them to improve Google's products and services is turned off.
For how Google handles information, see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The app has no setting to turn these statistics off. Once you delete the app from your device, nothing further is sent.

## 5. Sharing with third parties
We never sell your information. Apart from using Google's service for the statistics in Section 4, we do not provide it to third parties.

## 6. Deleting data
- You can remove your home in the app under Settings → Remove Home.
- Deleting the app from your device erases all information the app has stored on your device.
- The statistics in Section 4 are deleted automatically according to the Google Analytics retention setting.

## 7. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy as needed. If there are important changes, we will announce them in an app update or on this page.
