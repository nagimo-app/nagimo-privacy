# Privacy Policy (nagimo)

Last updated: 2026-11-06

[日本語](../)

nagimo ("the app") respects your privacy.
This policy explains the information the app handles.

## 1. Our approach: your current location and usage never leave your device
The app **never sends the current location it gets from your location permission, your registered places, lock settings, or app usage off your device**.
All of this is handled only on your device.

- The app has no servers and never sends or uploads this information to outside services.
- The free version shows ads when you pause (see "7. Advertising"). To show ads, device information is sent to the ad provider (Google).
- Apart from the ad system, it contains no analytics or crash reporting. It does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency).
- There are no accounts or sign-ins.

## 2. Information handled on your device
- **Registered places and areas**: the name, location (latitude and longitude), and radius of the places you register, such as home or work.
- **Lock settings**: the apps, categories, and websites to lock at each place, and their available times and days.
- **Current state**: whether you are at a registered place, when a pause ends, and whether you've used your first pause of the day.
- **Purchase status**: whether you have purchased nagimo Pro (purchases are handled by Apple; the app never receives payment information).
- **Usage milestones**: whether you have used your locked apps past milestones such as 30 minutes or 1 hour today, so the lock screen can let you know (cleared when the day changes).

This information is shared only between the app and its extensions (lock screen, schedule monitoring, and usage display), and never leaves your device.

## 3. Device permissions
- **Location (Always)**: used to detect when you enter or leave the places you registered, and switch locks automatically. "Always" is requested so this works even when the app is closed. Your location history is not stored and is never sent off your device.
- **Screen Time**: used to lock the apps and websites you choose, and to show the usage of your locked apps. iOS only provides information about your chosen apps in anonymized form. Usage is displayed using a mechanism provided by iOS; the app does not store or send it (except for the usage milestones above).

You can revoke these permissions at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

## 4. Sharing with third parties
We never provide or sell your information to third parties. Information sent to Google to show ads is described in "7. Advertising".

## 5. Deleting data
- You can remove registered places in the app under Settings → Places.
- Deleting the app from your device erases all information the app has stored.

## 6. Purchases
nagimo Pro (yearly or lifetime) is purchased through Apple's In-App Purchase. Payment information is handled by Apple, and the app never receives it.

## 7. Advertising
The free version shows Google AdMob video ads (rewarded ads) when you pause locks. nagimo Pro shows no ads.

- The app requests **only non-personalized ads**. Because it does not ask for tracking permission, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available.
- To show ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends Google: device identifiers (such as app- or developer-scoped IDs), your general region estimated from your IP address, records of ads you've seen and interacted with, and performance information (such as launch time and crash logs).
- The app never passes the current location from your location permission, your registered places, locked apps, or Screen Time usage to the ad system.
- For how Google handles information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 8. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy as needed. If there are important changes, we will announce them in an app update or on this page.
