FAQ

What should I set up first?

Follow the onboarding to allow Screen Time and Location (Always), then tap “Set Current Location as Home” while you’re at home. After that, choose the apps you don’t want to use at home under “Lock at Home”.

Why does the app need Location “Always”?

So it can detect when you arrive home or leave and switch locks, even when the app is closed. Your location is used only on your device and is never sent anywhere.

It says “Away” when I’m at home (or the other way around)

Location detection can be off by tens to hundreds of meters. Try widening your home area’s radius in Settings (150 m or more is recommended).

What if I really need to use an app?

Tap “Pause” on the home screen and choose 15 minutes, 1 hour, or until tomorrow. The lock comes back automatically when time’s up.

Apps aren’t being locked

Check in Settings that Screen Time and Location (Always) are allowed. Apps with available times aren’t locked during those times.

Usage isn’t shown, or differs from other apps

Usage shows only your locked apps, taken from iOS Screen Time. Check that Screen Time is allowed. It includes time used during available times and while paused. Because of how iOS counts usage, it may differ by a few minutes.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use the app without signing up or signing in. Your settings are stored only on your device.
